8. SCAN LIMITATIONS & DEFENSIVE IMPLICATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
During this assessment, the following technical limitations were observed:
1. Port Coverage: The standard scan evaluates the top 1,000 TCP ports. Non-standard
   ports (outside the top 1,000) or UDP services remain un-enumerated unless full-range
   (-p-) or UDP (-sU) scans are performed.
2. Firewall / Filtering: If host-based packet filters (e.g., iptables, UFW) are enabled
   or configured to drop packets silently, ports will appear as 'filtered' rather
   than 'open' or 'closed'.
3. OS Detection Heuristics: OS detection relies on TCP/IP stack behavior fingerprinting.
   If NAT or intermediate virtualization layers rewrite packet headers, detection
   accuracy can vary or report multiple candidate kernels.
4. Vulnerability Inference: Identifying an open port confirms communication readiness,
   not an exploitable vulnerability. A full assessment requires authenticated configuration
   review and dedicated vulnerability auditing tools.
